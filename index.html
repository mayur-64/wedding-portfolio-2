Frequently Asked

Questions.

Where are you based and how far can you travel?

Our studios are based in Lake Como, Italy, and New York City. Over 80% of our commissions take place globally — across Lake Como, the Amalfi Coast, Tuscany, Paris, Provence, Mallorca, the Scottish Highlands, and private estates across North America. Travel, lodging, and transport logistics are fully coordinated by our team and included transparently in your proposal.

How can we reserve our date and book you?

To secure your date, we require a countersigned digital commission agreement and an initial retainer of 30%. Because we accept a maximum of twenty weddings each calendar year, dates are secured strictly on a first-come, first-committed basis.

What are your signature deliverables?

Every collection is custom-tailored, but our signature features include a 5–8 minute Cinematic Poem Film, a 25–40 minute Director’s Feature Film, full multicam multiclip documentary edits of the Vows and Speeches in 4K UHD, an authentic Super 8 Kodak cine-film reel, an online private gallery of 800–1,200 curated and color-graded medium format photographs with full print release, and a handcrafted Japanese linen presentation box with custom USB and fine art archival prints.

How much do you charge and what are the payment terms?

Single-day commissions begin at €9,500 / $10,500, with comprehensive multi-day destination weekend collections averaging €14,000–€22,000 depending on location, team footprint (cinematographers, still photographers, Super 8 specialists, sound recordists), and bespoke album selections. Payment is split into three installments: 30% upon booking, 40% mid-season, and 30% one month prior to your celebration.

What are the delivery timelines?

Within 7 days of your wedding, you receive a curated 40-image sneak peek gallery and a 60-second vertical social teaser reel. The complete high-resolution still photography collection is delivered within 8 weeks. Your completed films, documentary edits, and archival physical heirloom box are delivered within 12 to 14 weeks.

How do you work on the wedding day?

Our philosophy is quiet, observant, and deeply respectful of the sacred emotional atmosphere. We do not stage artificial scenarios or interrupt authentic moments with cumbersome equipment. Instead, we move with effortless discretion, using natural light and subtle positioning to document the unscripted romance, laughter, and family bonds as they organically unfold.
